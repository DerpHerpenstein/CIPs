# Abstract
The current decentralized exchange (DEX) on Counterparty relies on manual order placement. Manual order placement is well-suited for assets with limited supply, but it becomes less efficient for assets with large supply which can lead to sparse liquidity and price volatility. This proposal addresses this issue by introducing a programmable, automated system for market making called Dex Market Makers. DMMs allow users to specify an asset pair, price range, spread, and order type (linear or logarithmic), which is then automatically decomposed into a series of discrete DEX orders. When these orders are filled, the system automatically creates new orders on the opposite side with a spread-adjusted price and credits the user with the spread amount. This continuous rebalancing ensures that liquidity is maintained and adjusted in response to market conditions.
